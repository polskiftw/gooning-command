UPDATE YOUR GITHUB REPOSITORY TO v0.2.3
=======================================

YOU ONLY REPLACE THREE FILES
----------------------------

1. app.py
2. settings.json
3. .github/workflows/download-cats.yml

Do not touch your secrets.
Do not replace worker/worker.js.
Do not delete anything from R2.

PART 1 — REPLACE app.py
-----------------------

1. Open your GitHub repository.
2. Click app.py.
3. Click the pencil/edit button.
4. Delete everything in the editor.
5. Open the new v0.2.3 app.py on your computer.
6. Copy all of it.
7. Paste it into GitHub.
8. Click Commit changes.

PART 2 — REPLACE settings.json
------------------------------

1. Go back to the Code tab.
2. Click settings.json.
3. Click the pencil/edit button.
4. Replace its contents with the new settings.json.
5. IMPORTANT: put your three real subreddit names back in.
6. Click Commit changes.

The new easy knobs are:

"posts_per_subreddit_per_scan": 50
    Check at most the newest 50 posts from each subreddit per run.

"reddit_request_delay_min_seconds": 2
"reddit_request_delay_max_seconds": 4
    Wait a random 2 to 4 seconds between Reddit/page requests.

"stop_after_consecutive_archived_posts": 15
    Stop scanning that source after 15 files in a row are already in history.

"reddit_429_backoff_seconds": 60
    If Reddit says "Too Many Requests," wait 60 seconds before retrying.

Leave those defaults alone unless you later have a reason to change them.

PART 3 — REPLACE THE WORKFLOW FILE
----------------------------------

1. Go back to the Code tab.
2. Open .github.
3. Open workflows.
4. Open download-cats.yml.
5. Click the pencil/edit button.
6. Replace its contents with the new v0.2.3 file.
7. Click Commit changes.

This changes the automatic schedule to every 15 minutes:

:03, :18, :33, and :48 each hour.

PART 4 — RUN IT
---------------

1. Click Actions.
2. Click Download cats to R2.
3. Click Run workflow.
4. Leave main selected.
5. Click the green Run workflow button.

You are done.
